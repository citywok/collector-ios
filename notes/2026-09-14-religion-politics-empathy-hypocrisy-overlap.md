# Religion × Politics × Empathy × Hypocrisy — evidence-correlated overlap map

**Date:** 2026-09-14 · **Type:** research note · Method: primary-source reviews with opened pages/effects; discourse tiers qualitative (no counts retrievable).

Every claim below is tagged: **STRONG** (multiple primary sources, numbers read), **PARTIAL** (single-tier evidence), **CONTESTED** (measures disagree in sign), **UNVERIFIED** (no data found).

## 1. Empathy by ideology

- Everyone empathizes ingroup-first: empathy bias r=.44 overall (Casey/Vanman/Barlow 2023, PSPB 51(5):678-700, PMC11930637, 4 preregistered vignette experiments, N=4,737). Liberal bias r=.52, conservative bias r=.36. **STRONG**
- Vignette-target empathy flipped: conservatives showed MORE empathy toward liberal targets than liberals toward conservative targets (cross-partisan asymmetry r=.21, Z=8.08); mediated by liberals' harsher moral judgments of conservatives. Caveat: target-specific self-report ratings of hypothetical vignettes — NOT behavioral. **STRONG as experimental-vignette result**
- Trait self-report empathy is liberal-higher (Iyer 2012; Hasson 2018 PSPB, US/Israel/Germany; Waytz 2016). **STRONG**
- MEG (Zebarjadi 2023 SCAN, N=55): TPJ vicarious-suffering response present in leftists, absent in rightists (r≈.30s vs self-report ideology and RWA). **CONTESTED/weak** (small N; self-report ideology predictor)
- Trait empathic concern does not depolarize — it *fuels* affective polarization, because sympathy flows to co-partisans (Simas, Clifford & Kirkland 2020, APSR 114(1):258-269). **STRONG**
- Net: "who feels more empathy" is measure-dependent; the measure-robust fact is ingroup-tilting on all measures. No standalone IRI×ideology meta-analysis exists.

## 2. Religiosity × doing-good

- Religiously observant Americans give and volunteer MORE, including for *secular* causes (Putnam/Campbell 2010, *American Grace*; Brooks 2003, income-controlled). Driver is congregational social networks, not belief content. Caveat: self-report survey data. **STRONG (direction), PARTIAL (mechanism)**
- Religious priming meta: g=.40, 929 effects, N=11,653 (Shariff et al. 2016 PSPR) — but publication-bias/p-curve contested post-replication-crisis; load as weak causal evidence. **PARTIAL**
- Belief-action gap measured in religion: extrinsic religiosity r=+.17 with racism, fundamentalism r=+.13, identification r=+.10, intrinsic r=-.07 (self-report only; behavioral gap for intrinsics), quest r=-.07 (tolerant); fundamentalism effect vanishes controlling authoritarianism; field effect r=+.20 (social-science outlets) vs r=-.10/-18 (religious outlets) (Hall/Matz/Wood 2010 PSPR, full PDF read). **STRONG for the structure of the gap**
- Disaffiliation reasons in Pew (2024 RLS / 2025 report): stopped believing 51%, religion unimportant 44%, drifted 42%; 47% dislike religious organizations, 30% bad experiences. **Hypocrisy is NOT a listed reason** — it is a narrative pin, not a data item. **STRONG**

## 3. Hypocrisy accounting

- Mechanism symmetric: identical behavior/law/criticism judged differently depending on target leader's party (London Business School counterfactual-hypocrisy study); reciprocal metadehumanization — each side exaggerates how dehumanized the other treats them (Moore-Berg 2020 PNAS); grandstanding (moral talk for status) is equally prevalent on both sides and correlates with narcissism, not ideology (Grubbs/Warmke/Tosi; Vox/PLOS.ONE). **STRONG (symmetric mechanism)**
- Target asymmetric — both ways: the loudest audit *term* ("virtue signaling") is aimed leftward in discourse (Wikipedia usage note); the loudest audit *waves* (evangelical-hypocrisy-at-Trump; "toxic empathy"/"empathy is a sin" — Stuckey, Rigney, Mohler) are aimed rightward/right-internal. In-group blindness: 12% of evangelicals say Christians are hypocritical vs 85% of young outsiders (Episcopal 2022 poll; Barna 87%/85%). **PARTIAL (qualitative discourse evidence)**
- Measurement split on who signals: the *grandstanding trait* is ideology-symmetric (Grubbs/Warmke/Tosi), but *victim/virtue-signaling scales* tilt liberal (Ok et al. 2021, "Signaling Virtuous Victimhood," J Business Ethics — Dark-Triad-linked, liberal-associated; Krispenz & Bertrams 2024, Personality and Individual Differences — virtuous moral signaling associated with left-wing self-identification). Scale scope matters; treat "which side signals" as CONTESTED rather than settled.
- Trump anchors: 81% of white evangelical voters chose Trump 2016; 72% approved Apr 2025 (Pew). Pew's own reason-data does not list hypocrisy as a driver (see above). **STRONG**

## 4. Party × religion demography (Pew RLS 2023-24, N≈36,900)

- Evangelical Protestants 23% of adults, ~70% R-lean; Black Protestants 72% D-lean; religiously unaffiliated ~28% of adults, 70% D vs 27% R mis-lean; Catholics ~50 R / 44 D. **PARTIAL-STRONG**
- Secular-Republicans cell: ~27% R-lean × ~28% nones ≈ 7-8% of adults — a very large block getting near-zero attention. Religious-Democrat blocks (Black Protestants, Hispanic Catholics, Jews, progressive Christians) are the biggest underreported cluster. **PARTIAL**

## 5. Discourse attention tiers (qualitative)

- HEAVY: "toxic empathy" (NYT interview Jul 2025, Atlantic Jun 2025, PBS, Guardian, Economist, Sojourners, Christianity Today), evangelical hypocrisy, "virtue signaling" as anti-left term.
- LIGHT: secular Republicans (repeated searches surfaced no major coverage), religious-Democrat blocks, conservative outgroup-empathy result, asymmetric-prejudice found effects (extrinsic r=+.17) vs the "fundamentalists are prejudiced" headline.

## 6. Over- vs under-weighted intersections

**Over-weighted (attention ≫ evidence-mechanism):**
1. "Liberals = the empathy party" as trait identity — rests on self-report scales; cross-partisan vignette empathy flipped, and APSR shows trait empathy fuels polarization regardless of wing.
2. "Hypocrisy explains religion's decline" — Pew's ranked reasons don't include it; it survives as rhetoric.
3. "Evangelical hypocrisy" as a standalone phenomenon — the 2025 "toxic empathy" turn is explicit doctrine re-prioritization (preached norm changed), which is apostasy-from-a-virtue, not classical hypocrisy; the true hypocrisy exposure (behavior gap on intrinsics) is worse for everyone.

**Under-weighted (evidence ≫ attention):**
1. Conservative cross-partisan empathy (r=.21) — the single most counter-narrative number retrieved; measure-dependent but N=4,737.
2. Religion as a behavioral giving/volunteering engine incl. secular causes (with the parochialism caveat: extrinsic-orientation prejudice coefficient stands after authoritarian control, only fundamentalism washes out).
3. Hypocrisy mechanism is symmetric (identical behavior judged by target's party; both sides exaggerate the other's dehumanization; grandstanding is ideology-blind narcissism) — nobody audits their own camp's claimed virtue.
4. Secular Republicans (~7-8% of adults) and religious Democrats (Black Protestants 72% D, etc.) — the real Venn bulk, absent from coverage.

## 7. Honest verdicts (post-audit corrections)

- Do not say "behaviorally" for the cross-partisan empathy flip (vignette ratings, not behavior).
- Do not say religion is "the largest" prosociality engine (no comparative effect-size evidence) — say "measurably larger giving/volunteering, network-driven, robust to income controls in Brooks."
- The religion-prejudice finding that survives hostile reading is the *extrinsic orientation* coefficient (r=+.17, k=22), not "religion=prejudice"; fundamentalism runs through authoritarianism.
- The symmetrical-mechanism / asymmetric-target frame for hypocrisy is the defensible contraction of the original asymmetric-accounting claim.

## Addendum — the leopardsatemyface hypothesis & the MAGA bisect (2026-09-14, later same day)

User hypothesis: "Republicans don't have empathy, hence leopardsatemyface." Assessment after targeted dig: the *phenomenon* (self-harming votes) is well supported; the *no-empathy cause* is not. Corrected mechanism, three verified components:

1. **Empathy exists but is threat-gated.** Cikara-lab line: empathic neural response is reduced to competitive outgroups, and rivals' suffering can activate ventral striatum (reward) correlated with desire to harm (Cikara/Bruneau/Saxe; Chang et al. 2016 review). Partisan schadenfreude is measurable and predicts candidate support (SciAm synthesis). This is the deficit's real shape — not absence, but re-targeting under competition.
2. **Moral metrics bend to accommodate the leader.** PRRI: white evangelicals saying an official who commits an immoral act can still serve ethically: 30% (2011) → 72% (Oct 2016) — the sharpest swing of any religious group (NPR/PRRI). "Unenlightened self-interest" (Bartels 2004): of respondents with the strongest conceivable personal reasons to keep the estate tax, 66% still favored repeal. Core driver of self-harming votes is identity-protective cognition (Kahan), i.e. motivated belief, not absence of fellow-feeling.
3. **Instrument audit holds.** Cross-partisan vignette empathy favored conservatives (r=.21, Casey et al. 2023 N=4,737); trait self-report favored liberals (Hasson 2018); no clean partisan asymmetry in blatant dehumanization (Martherus et al. 2021); compassion collapse runs on wealth (Piff), not party.

**MAGA bisect correction:** the map's center of gravity is not conservatism-as-type but the Trump movement as a distinct configuration: authoritarian predisposition as the unifying GOP variable (Hetherington & Weiler 2009/2018), status threat rather than economic hardship as the 2016 driver (Mutz 2018 PNAS), and a Christian-nationalist identity-religion bloc (Whitehead & Perry scale; PRRI: 10% Adherents + 20% Sympathizers vs 66% Skeptics/Rejecters). Leopardsatemyface evidence draws disproportionately from this bloc; that is a MAGA-plus-CN finding, not "Republicans don't have empathy."

**Correction to the causal religion evidence:** the Seshat/Whitehouse moralizing-gods *Nature* paper has been RETRACTED (Semantic Scholar marks the retraction; Turchin critique round). With religious-priming meta effects contested (Shariff 2016, publication-bias-flagged), the surviving causal story for religion's prosociality is the congregational-network/accessibility account (Putnam & Campbell; Brooks field data), not a belief-content mechanism.

**Supplemental findings (2026-09-14, second dig — 6 gap-closers):**

1. **No behavioral giving deficit by ideology (C11 upgraded).** Two primary checks: "Do Liberals Play Nice? Party/Ideology in Public Goods and Trust Games" (ResearchGate) — NO significant giving differences by party/ideology in structured economic games under inequality treatments; and the Berkeley Science-of-Generosity white paper — conservatives give more to religious charities than liberals, but the gap **disappears controlling church attendance**. Combined with Brooks: the ideological giving gap is a worship-attendance artifact, not a psychology artifact. "Conservatives behave less generously" has no surviving behavioral evidence.
2. **Christian nationalism correlates (C-addendum, partially verified).** CN + anti-immigrant attitudes verified (stronger among *religiously inactive*; attendance moderates — Wilson/Brewminate reviews); CN was strongest-or-second-strongest predictor in panel data of prioritizing economy/liberty over *protecting the vulnerable* during COVID (Whitehead & Perry, PMC7798614); CN → political-extremism/violence support (Brewminate review of peer-reviewed work). Direct CN↔empathic-concern scale study still not located — flag as open, not asserted.
3. **Religious left shrinkage verified (C9/C8 upgraded).** Ryan Burge 2026 *Vanishing Church* (America Mag): moderates and liberals are the ones leaving church; he traces the change to the 1990s (internet, televangelist backlash, rise of the religious right, atheism-stigma decay). Combined with the Christian Post/Brewmente read of Burge: religious-secular gap is *widening inside the GOP* (Brewminate "under the microscope") — so secular-Republican growth is real but not disproportional to religious-Republican consolidation.
4. **Secular political-engagement edge (yet another inversion).** Burge via GetReligion (2021): in 2018 survey data, **atheists were twice as likely as white evangelicals to donate to or work for a political candidate** — further confirming that "who acts generously" is domain-bounded, not group-held: seculars out-give evangelicals in political-giving, evangelicals out-give seculars in charitable-giving.
5. **Still unverified:** cruelty-as-strength primary experiment; quantitative discourse-attention counts (all curl-based probes dead); Saroglau/parochial-prosociality meta-effect size.
